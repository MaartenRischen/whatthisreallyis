What really is:

Short form video addiction

What it really is

People-watching.

Humans are built to watch other humans, and the feed is an endless supply of people who will never know you.

Then and now

For 300,000 years, the people you watched lived in your camp.

Not being able to stop watching is completely normal.

The 11 details
  1. People watch for hours a day.
  2. Many say they cannot stop.
  3. They feel worse afterwards.
  4. The videos are of people.
  5. Nobody in the feed knows the viewer.
  6. It mostly happens alone, often in bed at night.
  7. Each video costs one swipe.
  8. The feed has no end and is tuned to each viewer.
  9. It is free to watch; advertisers pay.
  10. Block one app and people move to the next.
  11. Teenagers do it most visibly.

This phenomenon in Cor 5 systems, each with its grade and age

M3Social Bonding

Cor grade: established, circuit about 200 Myr old wins here

In plain EnglishForms bonds with other humans. Pain-grade distress when they're gone.

The researchAttachment and co-regulation system. Includes grief mode. Two states: security (enables broadly) and grief (suppresses broadly).

Fully met byReliable human co-regulation through physical presence, in nested layers: about 5 intimate co-regulators, about 15 in the sympathy group, about 50 close social partners, about 150 face-to-face relationships. The innermost 5 provide most of the regulation, and what counts there is reliability, not count. The exact figures are contested (Lindenfors, Wartel & Lind 2021); the layering is what the evidence carries.

M14Reproductive Motivation

Cor grade: established, circuit about 500 Myr old

In plain EnglishThree components - desire, attraction, and pair bonding - that can work independently of each other. This system shapes much more of human behavior than reproduction itself.

The researchFisher's three-system model. Independent from M2 pursuit. HPG axis, not just brain circuits.

Fully met bySexual fulfillment within pair bond + pair bond stability + reproductive success.

M5Status Monitoring

Cor grade: established, circuit about 50-100 Myr old

In plain EnglishConstantly monitors where you stand relative to others.

The researchRank regulation. Shame, pride, submission, defeat, rank vigilance. Not optional - runs continuously.

Fully met byAcceptable position in a bounded, personally-known reference group on the order of 150 (an illustrative central tendency; the precise figure is contested per Lindenfors 2021). Earned prestige preferred over coerced dominance.

What cuts against it

  • Adults with full lives scroll for hours too. That points at how the feed is built more than at the empty evening.

    tested many timesE448 Schüll

  • Pooled studies find only a small link between teenagers' social media use and low mood (r about 0.11), so the hours may matter less than they look.

    tested many timesE103 meta-analysis, 12 studies

  • Cor ranks endless scrolling one rung above the bottom for the pursuit system, and flags that ranking as open to challenge.

    M2 Pursuit and exploration

  • The cause may run the other way: if everyone is scrolling, nobody is outside to join.

So now what

  1. Fill the evening first. A blocked app leaves the same empty hours.
  2. Watch together and talk about it.
  3. Screens are fine when the people on them know you.
