    The evolutionary angle: what your brain sees

    Hunter-gatherer bands did not put up with bosses. Anyone who tried to take charge got argued down, mocked or left behind. A leader on TV reads as a man in your camp giving orders, and anger is how you push back.

    The mismatch

    At the fire, the whole camp could argue a would-be boss down. A leader on TV can't hear you, and millions of others feel the opposite. The anger has nowhere to go, and a bill wants to call it a mental illness.

    So now what?

    1. Put the anger where it has an effect. Local politics, a campaign, a vote, a group that meets in person.
    2. Argue with people, not screens. Talk it through with people you know, including ones who disagree.
    3. Cut the coverage down. You can stay informed without every update.
