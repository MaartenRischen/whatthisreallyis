What really is:

Purpose

What it really is

A drive left with no target.

Cor argues that forager effort met its result within minutes to days, while much modern work breaks that link. It reads feeds, without testing it yet, as firing the wanting without giving the drive anything to finish.

Then and now

A forager dug, tracked or built something and saw the result within days.

Someone reaches the goal they worked toward for years and has nothing next to work on.

Sources: EEA-SUBS-02. Time scale: Hublin et al. 2017; Richter et al. 2017.Gold rings: the alarm is on

How it happens

  1. 1

    The pursuit drive pushes you to explore and work on real problems. In forager camps, effort and result sat close together: you dug, tracked or built and saw the outcome within days.

    The science says

    Cor's M2 resolves on engagement with real challenges that produce tangible outcomes, and SEEKING is a persistent exploratory drive (E397). Cor's row on forager work describes a short, visible loop from effort to result; it is argued and not yet tested.

  2. so2

    Much modern work breaks that loop: effort runs through long opaque chains, and rewards come detached in time from the effort.

    The science says

    Cor records that much modern work cuts the link between effort and a visible result, and that work time is clocked and continuous. The digital-work reading in E587 is a prediction and has not been tested.

  3. so3

    Then the goal arrives, or the job ends, or it turns into meetings all day. The drive is still running and has no real task in front of it.

    The science says

    Cor's M2 resolves on goal progress and real challenges with tangible outcomes. Cor's M6 ranks abstract work where effort does not visibly register as a mixed match, in the middle of its gradient.

    M2 Pursuit / Exploration, establishedM6 Controllability / Agency, established

    Not in Cor yet: a study showing that felt purpose drops after a long-held goal is reached or work ends.

  4. so4

    Cor reads feeds and endless scrolling as firing the wanting side of the drive without resolving it, a reading not yet tested.

    Each pleasure that fails to close the gap sends the drive back to looking.

    The science says

    In healthy humans, lowering dopamine cut the wanting for a drug and left the liking unchanged, across several studies (E133). Cor ranks idle scrolling and browsing near the bottom of M2's gradient. Cor reads apps and feeds as wanting without resolution, and that reading has not yet been tested case by case (E131).

  5. so5

    The search for purpose becomes another distant goal. Coaches sell it, people compare themselves with strangers who post about theirs, and the search itself becomes a source of stress.

    The search sets a new distant goal with no visible result, which repeats the second link.

    The science says

    Klinger's account, reported by Nesse, holds that low mood rises when a goal cannot be reached and the person cannot let it go; this is argued and not tested (E137). Cor's M5 places comparison by follower and like counts near the bottom of its gradient, a rung derived from the architecture.

What they say it isWhat it really is

lack of meaning or purpose

The pursuit drive has no real challenge in front of it that gives a result you can see.

no motive in life

The motive was one distant goal. Once that goal was reached, the drive had nothing left to work on.

hedonic treadmill

Feeds and endless scrolling, Cor reads without yet testing, fire the wanting without giving the outcome the drive resolves on.

purpose anxiety

The search for purpose has become a new distant goal with no visible result, and it is measured against strangers online.

Which system wins

  1. 1

    Oldest. Brainstem and hypothalamus, about 500 million years old

    The SEEKING system's dopamine circuits drive wanting and exploration toward resources, whether or not anything arrives to finish the pursuit.

    The pursuit drive ran on a distant goal for years; once pay, security or retirement arrived, the drive has no target and pleasures cannot close the loop. WinsThe energy budget will not spend effort where nothing real is left to move for, so leisure and pleasure leave the person flat.

  2. 2

    Limbic system, about 200 million years old

    The controllability system tracks whether actions produce results, and in rats passivity is the default until control is detected.

    Purpose is treated as a private quantity to find inside oneself, when it is what other people give back for being useful to them.The unnoticed background is work away from family, a day with no people who depend on you, and living far from kin, so nothing outside the job gives the day a shape.Effort that no longer visibly changes anything for anyone, such as meetings all day, leaves the agency system flat.

  3. 3

    Newest. Cortex, about 50 to 100 million years old, with human add-ons from the last few million

    Status monitoring compares your position against others, and Cor says it resolves in a group of people you know, which millions of online strangers are not.

    Ancestrally, purpose came from being needed by a few dozen people who knew you, whose regard you could see; a job among strangers gives none of that.

Simplified. The older the system, the harder its signal hits and the less it can learn that the people on a screen are not in the room.

The 12 details
  1. People feel their life lacks meaning or purpose, as if they are drifting without direction or anything to do with the life they have.
  2. It often arrives right after people reach a goal they worked toward for years, such as a well-paid job, financial security or retirement.
  3. Free time, comfort and pleasures such as watching Netflix, gaming, partying, dating or relaxing do not relieve it and can make the emptiness plainer.
  4. The lack of purpose comes with anxiety, and the search for a purpose itself becomes a source of stress.
  5. It hits people who are comfortable and secure: those with good pay, health, relationships and free time still report it.
  6. Among Gen Z, adults aged 19-28 lack meaning more than middle or high school students (40% vs. 22%) and lack purpose more (43% vs. 30%).
  7. People who feel they make a positive difference in others' lives are about three-to-four times more likely to feel purpose or meaning, and helping others is repeatedly reported to ease it.
  8. Paid work is the main thing people's days and identity are built around; when the work feels pointless, such as sitting in meetings all day, or when it ends, the sense of purpose goes with it.
  9. Without the structure of work, days lose their shape and blur together, and adding routines and reconnecting with people slowly brings direction back.
  10. Most people want work that helps others (79% of Gen Z), but only 56% of those employed who want such a job have one, with insufficient pay (49%) and emotionally draining work (46%) named as barriers.
  11. People around them say it is common and offer fixes: exercise, meditation, waking early, reading, hobbies, volunteering, relationships, religion or having kids, and people busy with kids are described as having no time to ask the question.
  12. Purpose is presented on social media and by coaching companies as a personal achievement to find and work toward, inviting comparison with people who post about having found theirs and selling coaching to get there.

M2Pursuit / Exploration

Cor grade: established, circuit about 500 Myr old wins here

In plain EnglishDrives you to explore, pursue goals, and engage with the world.

The researchGeneral-purpose seeking system. SCOPE CHANGE from v3: sexual motivation removed (now M14). M2's resolution conditions narrow to: engagement with real-world challenges producing tangible outcomes, goal progress, skill building, resource acquisition.

Fully met byEngagement with real challenges producing tangible outcomes.

M2 in Cor: every source and grade

M11Cooperation / Alliance

Cor grade: strongly supported, circuit about 2-5 Myr old

In plain EnglishReciprocal exchange, cheater detection, coalitional identity.

The researchReciprocity + norm enforcement + alliance dynamics. Absorbs old M10 (Cooperation) and M11 (Coalitional). NOTE: Tier 2 is a confidence grade, not an entailment status. M11 IS entailment-forced - convergence C11 forces it - and its four converging streams (Fehr & Gächter, Boehm, Tomasello, Trivers) strongly support it without reaching the top confidence tier.

Fully met byTrusted reciprocal network with norm enforcement within a group small enough for direct reputation tracking (on the order of ~150 - the same low-hundreds ceiling that bounds M3; the precise figure is contested per Lindenfors 2021). Norm enforcement requires repeated direct interaction where defectors can be identified, remembered, and sanctioned. Beyond that ceiling, cooperation requires institutional scaffolding (formal rules, third-party enforcement, shared fictions) rather than direct reciprocity. No shared fiction at the direct-reciprocity layer.

M11 in Cor: every source and grade

M3Social Bonding

Cor grade: established, circuit about 200 Myr old

In plain EnglishForms bonds with other humans. Pain-grade distress when they're gone.

The researchAttachment and co-regulation system. Includes grief mode (absorbed from old M9 GRIEF). Two states: security (enables broadly) and grief (suppresses broadly).

Fully met byReliable human co-regulation through physical presence, operating through nested social layers: ~5 intimate co-regulators (primary emotional/physical support), ~15 sympathy group, ~50 close social partners, ~150 coherent face-to-face relationships (Dunbar number - outer cognitive ceiling). Each layer represents qualitatively different relationship types with different obligations and investment requirements. The innermost ~5 provide the majority of social regulation; the critical variable at this layer is reliability, not count. The ~150 outer figure is an illustrative central tendency, not a hard limit: its derivation from the primate neocortex-ratio regression is contested (Lindenfors, Wartel & Lind 2021). What the evidence carries is the nested layering and the reliability-over-count point, not the exact rung figures; the layered structure and its low-hundreds clustering are separately observed in phone, Christmas-card, and Facebook data.

Cor grade: established, circuit about 200 Myr old

In plain EnglishTracks whether your actions produce results. Default is passivity - agency must be learned.

The researchvmPFC-DRN controllability circuit. Learned helplessness is the DEFAULT; agency is the override. Critical for breaking cascades.

Fully met byGenuine action-outcome feedback. Decision latitude.

M5Status Monitoring

Cor grade: established, circuit about 50-100 Myr old

In plain EnglishConstantly monitors where you stand relative to others.

The researchRank regulation. Shame, pride, submission, defeat, rank vigilance. Not optional - runs continuously.

Fully met byAcceptable position in a bounded, personally-known reference group on the order of ~150 (an illustrative central tendency, not a hard number; the precise figure is contested per Lindenfors 2021). Earned prestige preferred over coerced dominance (Henrich & Gil-White distinction - needs explicit treatment).

What cuts against it

  • In the Gallup survey, feeling that you make a positive difference to others was one of the strongest predictors of meaning, which fits a reading built on being needed by others as well as this one.

  • Cor's records describe ancestral food work as collaborative and interdependent, so the missing piece may be the people the work fed more than the challenge itself.

    argued, not yet testedE239 Tomasello 2014M11 Cooperation / Alliance, strongly supported

  • Gen Z adults aged 19-28 report lacking meaning more than teenagers, and most of them have not reached any long-held goal yet.

Not in Cor yet

  • Reaching a long-held goal, or retiring, is followed by a drop in felt purpose.

    Study: A longitudinal panel that measures purpose before and after retirement or a major financial goal, such as the Health and Retirement Study.

  • Making a difference to others causes a sense of purpose.

    Study: Gallup and Making Caring Common 2026, Voices of Gen Z (correlational), plus an experiment that assigns people to helping tasks.

So now what

  1. Take on one task with a result you can see within days, such as building, fixing, teaching or growing something.
  2. Choose work or volunteering where specific people you know use what you make or do.
  3. Measure your progress against your own last month and leave out the people posting about their purpose.

What would prove this wrong

Take people who have just reached a long-held goal or retired. If those who take on a new real task with a result they can see within days report no more purpose after six months than those who only add leisure, this page is wrong.

Cor grades the split between wanting and liking in healthy humans as tested many times (E133). Its extension to apps and feeds is graded untested (E131). The seven-system map behind SEEKING is graded contested (E11).

Strongest objectionPurpose may come from mattering to other people more than from having a challenge. A retiree with a hard, visible hobby can still feel empty if nobody needs them.
