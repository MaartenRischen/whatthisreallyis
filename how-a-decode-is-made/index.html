The three checks, for every finding in Cor, the evidence record behind this site:

  1. Every finding is checked against the study's full text or abstract. Summaries, search snippets and Wikipedia don't count.
  2. The finding says no more than the study does.
  3. Every grade names the strongest study behind it and that study's main weakness.

A grade never goes up because it would help a page. A finding that failed to replicate is marked down, however widely it is cited. Cor keeps records of the evidence against itself, publishes the strongest attacks on its own work and names the results that would kill it. It's, in a word, really good science.

What every decode starts from

Seven starting points

They are stated here so you can reject them knowingly.

  1. 1

    Survival and reproduction come first.Every action, feeling, want and habit comes from a system that helped your ancestors stay alive, have children, or help their relatives do it. "I just like it" never ends an explanation.

  2. 2

    The measure is how humans lived for nearly all of their history.That life shows what humans need. Nobody has to go back to it. What counts is what that life supplied, never the form it took.

  3. 3

    The human works.A reaction is a signal about the person's situation. The cause sits in a world that stopped supplying what the systems were built for.

  4. 4

    What people say about themselves is data.A stated reason, a label, a diagnosis or a self-description gets decoded and is never adopted.

  5. 5

    A modern number is compared with how humans lived, never with another modern group.In a study of modern people, "normal" already means the mismatched life.

  6. 6

    Only a clear physical defect is a disease, found or not yet found.A marker that only shows how sensitive a person is to stimuli and mismatch is no defect. Where no defect is the cause, the reaction is a normal human response to a mismatched world. Genes set how easily it comes and how strong it is. No label is left out of this, psychosis and bipolar included.

  7. 7

    Cor decides.A decode is right when it follows from Cor's records. Nobody's opinion settles it, the founder's included.

The 14 steps

Each step shows what it did on one real page, Friendship, which completed all 14 steps on 23 November 2026. Where a step leaves something you can see on a decode page, that is named too. In the steps, "the writer" is the AI that writes the decode.

Read itSteps 1 to 3

1

Save the source

The writer saves the thing being decoded, as a working link or word for word, and gives every detail in it a number. For a word like friendship that means four or five different cases. The numbered list is what the decode has to explain.

On the pageThe list of details.

FriendshipA reader who had never seen the page gathered five first-person accounts: making a friend as an adult, a friend who never reaches out, money lent to a friend of 22 years, a best friendship that faded after a move, and a friend who showed up in a crisis. Together they hold 235 numbered details. The page lists nine that the accounts show, starting with "Losing a friend hurts."

2

Ask what the thing is

The thing is either someone's own account of their life or something a business made to sell. If it was made to sell, the decode asks who sells, who pays, and what was blown up past real life. Every decode also asks who earns money from the reaction and who earns money from the wrong answers.

On the page"Who sells the wrong answers".

FriendshipThe page names friendship apps, a site that rents out friends by the hour, and AI companion apps. It adds: "No one earns money when two neighbours help each other move."

3

The writer rewrites the modern word as what the human animal is doing or missing, and ties it to a system in Cor and to Cor's record of how humans lived. It searches Cor by what a thing does, never by its label.

They decide what a page says, so they are public

The rules for the writing

  • The claim is said flat. The check against Cor never adds a "maybe". It sorts a claim four ways: Cor says it in other words, Cor has a gap, the checker misread, or Cor says the opposite. Only the last changes the claim.
  • A missing study is a gap in Cor. It goes on Cor's list of what it needs. It is never printed beside a claim as a weakness.
  • A study's size, year and weak points stay with its reference. The evidence against a page sits in one place on that page, complete.
  • An animal study never stands alone as proof about people. The line says why it carries over: which system it is, that humans share it and since when by Cor's record, and what Cor holds on the same system in people. A shared system is a reason to expect the same in people, and the line states it as that. Where Cor has neither a record of the shared system nor a study in people, the animal finding comes off the page.
  • A reading is stated bluntly as this project's position. It is never presented as a measured result.
  • Between two correct lines the more provocative one wins.
  • What "everyone knows" is a claim to check. So is what a guideline says, and so is what the AI remembers. None of them stands until the primary studies are read. The same check is run on our own claims.
  • On medication a page says the same thing every time, whole. The drug gets replaced by the real thing, such as people who need you and give back. The dose comes down while the real thing comes in. Where stopping suddenly is dangerous, as with certain drugs and alcohol, never stop suddenly, and do it with a doctor.

Who does what

AI writes the decodes, and Cor decides whether they are right

AI compiles the research and writes the decodes. The second reader in step 8 and the outside reader in step 11 are separate AI readers. The second reader gets the source and the starting points, and none of the writer's reasoning. The outside reader gets the page and Cor, and did not write the page.
