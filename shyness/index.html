What it really is

Being careful around strangers.

Shyness is being careful with people who don't know you yet. You were built for a life where you knew nearly everyone. Now you spend the day among strangers.

The part of you that makes you careful is 460 million years old.Learn moreClose
Humans have existed for 300,000 years, and for nearly all of that time you knew almost everyone you met. A stranger could get you killed. Then people started farming and settling down, and villages grew into towns and cities. That began 12,000 years ago. Now you deal with strangers all day, at work, in shops and on the phone. You get careful every time, and people call that a flaw.

So now what

  1. See the same people in the same place every week. You stop being shy with people you know.
  2. Take one person you trust to anything new.
  3. Get to events early, while the room is still nearly empty.

If you take medication for anxiety: where no test shows a physical disease, the aim is to replace the drug with the real thing: people you see often enough that they know you. The drug goes as that comes in. Bring the dose down with a doctor while you build it. It doesn't have to be the doctor who prescribed it. Some drugs are dangerous to stop at once, so never stop suddenly.

The 8 details
  1. It shows up with strangers and fades with people you know.
  2. Babies show it from the second half of their first year.
  3. It is strongest when you are being judged: speeches, interviews, dates.
  4. Your heart races and you freeze.
  5. Shyness and wanting company are separate traits.
  6. It fades as a stranger becomes familiar.
  7. It runs in families.
  8. A trusted person beside you reduces it.

What would prove this wrong

If shy people are as shy with someone they have known for a year as they were on the first day, this page is wrong.

Adults felt less shy after ten minutes of getting to know a stranger.

Strongest objection"Plenty of people aren't shy at all." People differ in how careful they are, the same way they differ in height. It runs in families.
