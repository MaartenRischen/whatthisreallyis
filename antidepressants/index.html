Antidepressants

What it really is

Pills that barely beat a sugar pill.

In trials antidepressants beat a dummy pill by about 2 points on a 52-point scale. Whatever made you low is still there.

Humans go low when their people are missing, when they lose standing or when effort stops working. For 300,000 years the people around you saw it and closed in. Today a doctor names it depression and prescribes a pill.

So now what

  1. Find out what the low mood is about: missing people, lost standing or effort that shows no result.
  2. Put the same few trusted people in the same room every week.
  3. Join a small group where your effort shows a result you can see that week.

If you take antidepressants: where no blood test or scan shows a physical disease, the goal is to come off them. Work on the cause at the same time as a slow taper supervised by your doctor. Never stop suddenly. Stopping some drugs at once is dangerous.

The 9 details
  1. Doctors prescribe them for low mood that lasts for weeks.
  2. No blood test or scan comes first.
  3. Many users report feeling less of everything.
  4. In trials they beat a dummy pill by about 2 points on a 52-point scale.

  7. England counts 44 percent more patients in its poorest areas than in its richest.

What would prove this wrong

If people whose effort pays off again and who have trusted people around them stay as low without the pills as they were before, this page is wrong.

A study can settle this.

Strongest objection"The pills beat a dummy pill in 522 trials." They do. A reanalysis of those trials put the gap at about 2 points on a 52-point scale, measured at about eight weeks. The trials don't record what the low mood was about.
